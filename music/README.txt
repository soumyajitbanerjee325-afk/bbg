To add background music:
1. Place an MP3 file named "background.mp3" in this folder
2. The music will automatically play when the website loads
3. Recommended: Soft instrumental piano/ambient music, 1-3 minutes long, loopable
4. Keep volume low (the player sets it to 30% by default)

Free music sources:
- https://pixabay.com/music/
- https://www.chosic.com/free-music/
- https://freemusicarchive.org/
- YouTube Audio Library

The music player will gracefully handle missing audio files.
